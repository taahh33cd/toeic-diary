In local news, the abandoned shoe factory in the central business district is finally getting a makeover. The building has been empty for three years since the factory moved to its new, larger space south of town. After hearing many proposals, the town council voted last night to sell the building to developer Matthew Hughes, who will convert it into family housing: ten modern, comfortable units with a parking garage underground.
