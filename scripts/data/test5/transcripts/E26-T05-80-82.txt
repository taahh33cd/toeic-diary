The Novikov Award is named after Maksim Novikov, the founder of Novikov Aviation. It is given each year to a company that has made outstanding contributions to the aviation industry. The company chosen for the award this year was frustrated by the lack of qualified job applicants and decided to do something about it. Zenith Aviation's apprenticeship program has trained hundreds of workers for careers in aircraft maintenance and repair. Dozens of firms nationwide have copied the program.
