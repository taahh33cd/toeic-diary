Our next story is about Ferndale Valley. This area is well known as one of the windiest places in the region, and one company wants to take advantage of that natural energy source. Breeze Capture Incorporated hopes to install dozens of wind turbines by the end of next year. The company is looking for local farmers who are interested in leasing a portion of their land for the project. Not only will a fee be paid for the use of the land, but participants will also receive compensation for the energy generated by the turbines. For more information, please email info@breezecapture.com.
