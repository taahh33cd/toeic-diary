A market research firm has reported that the department store chain Willoughby is partnering with the beauty retailer Rossi. By the end of the year, Rossi stores will be in place at 500 Willoughby locations. Currently, most of the beauty retailer's sales come from small shops in urban areas. By joining forces with Willoughby, Rossi hopes to meet its goal of expanding its customer base by entering suburban markets. To accommodate the new retail spaces for Rossi, participating Willoughby stores will start renovations in July.
