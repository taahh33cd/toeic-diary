Are you looking for a convenient way to get around the city? The city of Lakepoint offers on-demand transportation in several of our neighborhoods. Just download the Lakepoint City application onto your mobile phone. Once you make an appointment on the app, a shuttle will arrive within fifteen minutes. This easy-to-use service will take you to any destination within the service area, like your doctor's office or the library. And we've recently expanded service to Westbrook, so residents can take the shuttle to the soccer stadium.
